3. **Capacity is limited.** A borrowing limit is only as large as the committed quotes and Reserve USDC behind it. Quotes can be consumed, and withdrawn after their notice period.
4. **Position Notes and the Nectar Reserve are testnet software.** Position Notes (NEP-171 tokens for quotes, loans and Reserve deposits) and the Nectar Reserve pool are built and tested in a sandbox and run only on NEAR testnet with mock assets. A Note carries authority over its record and has no monetary value. A Reserve deposit is not an investment offer: the "realised fee APR (trailing 7 days)" is a historical testnet figure, never a promised return. Transfers of a Note are refused while a settlement is pending.
