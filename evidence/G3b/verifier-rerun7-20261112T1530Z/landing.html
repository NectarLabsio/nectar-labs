Lenders price the exit, not the asset.

Example. Follow one position through four states. The numbers are the example from the hero: 300 mock NVDAx at $185, with $28,000 already drawn against the asset.

  1. A tokenized stock can trade at fair value all week and have no buyer on Saturday night. Lenders price that exit, so the limit stays low.

  2. Market makers escrow USDC behind quotes that can't leave without notice. Add the Reserve and verified DEX depth, and the same 300 shares support up to 70%.

  3. When the market closes or the price is older than 15 minutes, DEX depth drops to zero and the haircut widens to 25%. Nothing outside the contract can switch the session back on.

  4. If health falls below 1, collateral goes to a committed quote at no more than its stated discount. The Reserve fills only after every quote is used.

Transfer the note, transfer the position.

The rule is short enough to read in one sitting.

Agents get the same surface: the Feed MCP server returns the exact transaction to sign, and an agent that refers a borrower earns half of the 0.3% origination fee.

Agent quick start
// Borrow limit for one asset (spec §4.2). Integers only: _e6 USD, bps.pub fn borrow_limit_e6(&self, a: &Asset) -> u128 {    let weekend = !self.guard.session_open(a);    let h_bps = if weekend { a.h_weekend_bps } else { a.h_session_bps }; // 2500 / 1000 on testnet    let d = if weekend { 0 } else { a.dex_depth_e6 };    let backing = a.quotes_e6 + a.reserve_e6 + d;           // Q + R + D    let by_exit = backing * (10_000 - h_bps) / 10_000;    let by_ltv  = a.value_e6() * a.ltv_max_bps / 10_000;  // 70% on testnet    // the exit term is shared by every loan in the asset    by_ltv.min(by_exit.saturating_sub(a.total_debt_e6))}

// Weekend guard: fails closed, reopens only on its own calendarfn session_open(&self, a: &Asset) -> bool {    let now = env::block_timestamp_ms();    let fresh = now - a.price_ts_ms <= 15 * 60_000;    // 15 minutes    self.nyse.is_open(now) && fresh && a.conf_ok()}// No method lets a keeper or owner force a session open.

What you don't have to trust

The keeper

A function-call key limited to a few methods. It cannot attach deposits, choose prices or supply hashes, and it holds no remote-chain authority.

The price

Stale or wide prices are rejected and the weekend guard fails closed. TEE output is verifiable: accepted only from a pinned, attested program and clamped on-chain.

Remote deposits

No remote loan pays out until an attested read of the vault matches the key NEAR derived for that position.

Questions market makers and borrowers ask

Can't find yours? Write to the address below.

Is any of this on mainnet?

No. NEAR testnet and Solana devnet, with mock tokens. Mainnet needs an audit, a DAO owner with a timelock, and issuer approval for real tokenized stocks.

Why mock collateral?

No tokenized equity exists on NEAR testnet. The mock token has the same interface (NEP-141, 6 decimals), and every page says "mock" where you would first meet it.

What does a Shield guarantee?

It opens your loan at the weekend limit for a fixed window so the limit can't be cut mid-weekend. If the price gaps, committed buyers take collateral at no more than their stated discount. It does not cover a fall in the stock's price.

What happens on weekends and holidays?

When the market is closed or the price is older than 15 minutes, the contract sets DEX depth to zero and widens the haircut on its own. It reopens only with a fresh price inside its own NYSE calendar.

Who buys my shares in a Profit-Lock?

Committed buyers who opted into voluntary fills, at their discount, under a daily cap. You see the units sold and kept before you sign.

How does the Reserve earn?

A share of borrower premiums. The Reserve page shows realised trailing APR and never promises a rate; exiting takes 7 days' notice.

What if the verifiable TEE pricing or the oracle is down?

Settlement prices at the quote's ceiling and says so. The oracle falls back to an owner price with a visible badge. Both paths are tested and disclosed.

Can the keeper steal anything?

No. It cannot attach deposits or pass prices or hashes, and holds no remote-chain authority. The contract builds every message and re-checks the trigger against its own oracle state.

Where is the code?

Open source under MIT once released. pnpm demo:testnet reads the testnet contract without any keys, and the docs cover the contract, the Feed REST and MCP endpoints and the runbook.
