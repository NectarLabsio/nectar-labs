# WO-33b design evidence (Markets, Borrow, Shield, Position)

Files: `{feed,borrow,shield,position}-{1440,400}-{dark,light}.png` (reduced motion, full page, mocked v2 views with the artboard demo numbers as fixtures). Generated by `apps/web/e2e/design-app.spec.ts`.

- `/borrow` — differs from Borrow: "Confirm in wallet" opens the existing sign modal instead of the wallet, because every write must show msg, gas and deposit there; adds a flag-line slider (pre_liq_ltv_bps is part of the msg); Standard is the default.

# WO-33c design evidence (Portfolio, Earn, Curator, Agents, Market)

Files: `{portfolio,earn,reserve,console,agents,market}-{1440,400}-{dark,light}.png` (reduced motion, full page, mocked v2 views with the artboard demo numbers as fixtures). Generated by `apps/web/e2e/design-app.spec.ts`.

- `/notes` — differs from Portfolio: "Can still borrow" is the limit on collateral still in the wallet, because nectar-core has no draw-more on an open loan (the artboard's $19,175 assumes one); net value also counts quote escrow; Notes tab keeps the contract-drawn SVGs, with Transfer and Share on `/note/[id]`; Activity lists what the views store (one row per loan, quote, Reserve Note), since repayments and fills are events; Reserve rows have Give notice / Exit and no Claim (no such call).
- `/quote`, `/quote/new` — differs from Earn: the ceiling slider stops below the Reserve discount, which the contract enforces (the artboard's 5.00% cap with a 4.00% Reserve cannot be funded); a new quote stands behind equal ceilings (oldest first in `lib.rs`; the artboard puts it ahead); voluntary fills default off (WO-26b); notice presets come from config (7 d, 30 d; no 14); "From the last 7 days" is derived from the Reserve's `fees_7d`, the only fee history on chain, and reads n/a without a Reserve.
- `/reserve` — same composer in Reserve mode; adds R_c, in notice, share price and collateral held, and Reserve Notes sit in "Your commitments".
- `/console` — differs from Console: Force closed signs through the sign modal directly; Set multiplier and Announce are inline forms; Reserve, referrer, remote and P0/quoter owner controls stay below under "Contract controls"; the Max LTV slider starts at the static LTV (contract rule); example rows scale with the oracle price; Evidence is read from docs/EVIDENCE.md at build, Base shows "Pass, local fork"; keeper status shows the indexer, the only signal available.
- `/agents` — differs from Agents: the run replays the recorded fixture (1 NVDAx, $10, no Shield), not the artboard's 50 NVDAx Weekend Shield, and is labelled an example; Connect shows the real local commands, not `[feed host]` placeholders; `get_note` and `build_self_settle` are listed but answer not_available; the x402 walkthrough stays as its own panel.
- `/feed/[symbol]` — differs from Market: Activity is from contract state, not example events; oracle chip is the existing OracleBadge; the headline reads 389.94, not 389.95, because units are truncated.
- Contrast (light theme, tokens unchanged): `--accent` on `--bg2` is 4.0:1, so links and positive badges inside `.nx-well`, and positive figures inside `.nx-code`, use `--accent-hover`.
