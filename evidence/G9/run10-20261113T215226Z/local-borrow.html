Risk context from your notes

Paste corporate-action or risk notes. The model summarises them without prices or amounts. Answers come from NEAR AI Cloud and are shown only if every required check passes. In your browser: EIP-191 signature recovery, request/response hashes, that the signed request is the one built from your notes for the announced model, and the report_data/nonce binding. On this server: the Intel TDX quote (DCAP, via dcap-qvl) and, in live mode, NVIDIA GPU attestation (NRAS). The checks prove where the text came from, not that it is right. Testnet, mock collateral.
