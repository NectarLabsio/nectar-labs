Why limits are low, and what changes

Lenders price the exit, not the asset.

A tokenized stock may trade at fair value all week and have no buyer on Saturday night. Lending protocols respond with low LTV because the liquidity they would sell into can leave at any moment. Nectar Labs replaces that revocable depth with escrowed quotes that cannot leave without notice, and lets the limit follow the commitment.

Cramér limit
Bc = min( ltvstatic + uplift · Qc , ltvmax ) × collateral value

Qc is the sum of live Pappus quote remainders for the asset. It drops the moment a buyer gives notice, so new loans never lean on exiting liquidity while open loans stay backed through the notice window.

Pappus quote
  • Buyer escrows USDC in the contract and names an asset, a size and a worst-case discount (max_discount_bps).
  • A notice period (24 h or 7 d) must pass between initiate_quote_withdrawal and complete_quote_withdrawal.
  • Borrowers' premiums accrue to the quote through an O(1) fee index; fees are claimable any time.
  • When a position fails, the buyer takes collateral at the clearing discount, never worse than the ceiling they set.

Settlement

Four steps, no trusted keeper

The keeper only passes a position id and relays results. The contract decides the price, builds the message and asks the MPC network to sign it. A Solana program checks that signature before releasing anything.

01 · FLAG

Health falls below 1

Any keeper calls trigger_preliquidation(position_id) with a function-call key. No deposit, no price, no hash.

nectar-core · NEAR testnet
02 · PRICE

TEE computes the discount

A pinned OutLayer program reads private market-maker parameters and returns disc_bps, clamped to the quote's ceiling. Falls back to the ceiling if the TEE is unavailable, and says so.

outlayer.testnet · attested
03 · SIGN

Chain Signatures signs the order

The contract builds NECTAR_SETTLE_V1|… itself, hashes it and calls v1.signer for an Ed25519 signature (domain 1). The keeper never sees a key.

v1.signer-prod.testnet · MPC
04 · SETTLE

Solana verifies, then pays

A transaction with an Ed25519 verify instruction followed by nectar_vault.settle; the program checks the preceding instruction through the Instructions sysvar, pays the buyer, seizes the units, and the keeper relays the result home.

nectar_vault · Solana devnet

Security and privacy

What you do not have to trust

The keeper

Holds a function-call key limited to three methods. Cannot attach deposits, cannot choose prices, cannot supply hashes. The contract pays MPC and TEE fees from its own reserve.

The price

Oracle reads are rejected when stale or when confidence is wide; the UI shows the source and age. TEE output is accepted only from a pinned program hash and clamped on-chain.

Your data

Market-maker parameters never leave the enclave. Copilot answers are TEE-signed and verified in your browser. Analytics load only after consent; wallet addresses are never joined to IPs.

Every deployment and test run is recorded with commands, outputs and explorer links on the Evidence page. Unaudited testnet software; mock collateral.

FAQ

Questions market makers and borrowers ask

Is any of this on mainnet?

No. NEAR testnet and Solana devnet only, with mock USDC and mock NVDAx. Mainnet needs an audit, a DAO owner with a timelock and real tokenized-equity routes; that is the roadmap.

Why mock collateral?

No tokenized equity exists on NEAR testnet and 1Click lists no equity route today. The mock token has the same interface (NEP-141, 6 decimals) as the real thing, and every page says "mock" where you would first meet it.

What happens if the TEE or the oracle is down?

Settlement prices at the quote's max_discount_bps and emits quoter_fallback; the oracle falls back to an owner-proposed price with a visible badge. Both paths are permanent code, tested, and disclosed.

Can the keeper steal anything?

No. It cannot attach deposits, cannot pass prices or hashes, and holds no Solana authority. The contract builds the settlement preimage and the MPC network signs it. The Solana program refuses any settle without a matching Ed25519 verification immediately before it.

Why would a market maker commit?

Because a bounded, time-limited bid at a discount they chose is paid for continuously by borrower premiums, and the inventory logic that sets their real clearing price stays private inside the enclave.

Where is the code and how do I run it?

Open source, MIT. Five commands from a fresh clone; pnpm demo:testnet reads the live contract without any keys. The docs have the contract reference, the Feed REST and MCP endpoints and the runbook.
