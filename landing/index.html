Lenders price the exit, not the asset.

Follow one position through four states. The numbers are the example from the hero: 300 mock NVDAx at $185, with $28,000 already drawn against the asset.

  1. A tokenized stock can trade at fair value all week and have no buyer on Saturday night. Lenders price that exit, so the limit stays low.

  2. Market makers escrow USDC behind quotes that can't leave without notice. Add the Reserve and verified DEX depth, and the same 300 shares support up to 70%.

  3. When the market closes or the price is older than 15 minutes, DEX depth drops to zero and the haircut widens to 25%. Nothing outside the contract can switch the session back on.

  4. If health falls below 1, collateral goes to a committed quote at no more than its stated discount, priced in a TEE and signed by NEAR Chain Signatures. The Reserve fills only after every quote is used.

Position Notes, NEP-171

Transfer the note, transfer the position.

  • Every quote, loan and Reserve deposit is a Note on NEAR. Its owner is the one who can withdraw, claim fees or receive leftover collateral.
  • The contract draws the picture from live state: LTV, health, backing, multiplier.
  • Transfers are blocked while a position is settling, so a payout can't be redirected mid-flight.
  • Share a Note to X from its page. Nothing posts without you.

Anyone can recompute a limit.

The rule is short enough to read in one sitting. Agents get the same surface: the Feed MCP server returns the exact transaction to sign, and an agent that refers a borrower earns half of the 0.3% origination fee.

// Borrow limit for one asset (spec §4.2). Integers only: _e6 USD, bps.pub fn borrow_limit_e6(&self, a: &Asset) -> u128 {    let weekend = !self.guard.session_open(a);    let h_bps = if weekend { a.h_weekend_bps } else { a.h_session_bps }; // 2500 / 1000    let d = if weekend { 0 } else { a.dex_depth_e6 };    let backing = a.quotes_e6 + a.reserve_e6 + d;           // Q + R + D    let by_exit = backing * (10_000 - h_bps) / 10_000;    let by_ltv  = a.value_e6() * a.ltv_max_bps / 10_000;  // 70%    // the exit term is shared by every loan in the asset    by_ltv.min(by_exit.saturating_sub(a.total_debt_e6))}

What you don't have to trust

The keeper

A function-call key limited to a few methods. It cannot attach deposits, choose prices or supply hashes, and it holds no Solana or Base authority.

The price

Stale or wide prices are rejected and the weekend guard fails closed. TEE output is accepted only from a pinned program and clamped on-chain.

Remote deposits

No remote loan pays out until an attested read of the vault matches the key NEAR derived for that position.

Questions market makers and borrowers ask

Can't find yours? Write to the address below.

Is any of this on mainnet?

No. NEAR testnet, Solana devnet and Base Sepolia only, with mock tokens. Mainnet needs an audit, a DAO owner with a timelock, and issuer approval for real tokenized stocks.

What does a Shield guarantee?

It opens your loan at the weekend limit for a fixed window so the limit can't be cut mid-weekend. If the price gaps, committed buyers take collateral at no more than their stated discount. It does not cover a fall in the stock's price.

What happens on weekends and holidays?

When the market is closed or the price is older than 15 minutes, the contract sets DEX depth to zero and widens the haircut on its own. It reopens only with a fresh price inside its own NYSE calendar.

Who buys my shares in a Profit-Lock?

Committed buyers who opted into voluntary fills, at their discount, under a daily cap. You see the units sold and kept before you sign.

What if the TEE or the oracle is down?

Settlement prices at the quote's ceiling and says so. The oracle falls back to an owner price with a visible badge. Both paths are tested and disclosed.

Can the keeper steal anything?

No. It cannot attach deposits or pass prices or hashes, and holds no authority on Solana or Base. The contract builds every message and the vaults refuse anything without a matching signature.

Where is the code?

Open source under MIT once released. pnpm demo:testnet reads the live contract without any keys, and the docs cover the contract, the Feed REST and MCP endpoints and the runbook.
